# Copy

Voice from `BRAND.md`: dry, witty, understated. Scandinavian deadpan. The reader is a 28-year-old Stockholmer who decides at 6 PM.

- Short. Cut the sentence in half, then once more.
- No exclamation marks, no "unforgettable", "amazing", "don't miss". Enthusiasm is implied.
- Errors sound human and calm: "Couldn't reach the grid. Try again.", not "Error loading events."
- Empty states nudge: "Nothing here. Try loosening the filters."
- Light irony is fine, sarcasm aimed at the user isn't.
- Write Swedish as Swedish, not translated English. It should sound like a Stockholmer texting. Same tone, not word-for-word.
- Swedish is the default for email. Every string exists in both `en` and `sv`.
- Legal, privacy and consent text: plain and precise first, voice second.

Before shipping copy, ask: would a Stockholmer roll their eyes? Can a word go?
